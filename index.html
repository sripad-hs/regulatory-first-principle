Module 02 · Concept

How many regulatory bodies are there?

Roughly 190 countries each have some form of medicines regulator — but a handful of major agencies set the standards that ripple globally through reliance and harmonisation.

RegionAuthority
United StatesFDA — Food and Drug Administration
European UnionEMA + national competent authorities
United KingdomMHRA
JapanPMDA
ChinaNMPA
IndiaCDSCO under the DCGI

ICH (International Council for Harmonisation) is not a regulator itself — it's where major agencies agree on common guidelines like the CTD format, so a sponsor doesn't have to reinvent the dossier for every country.

Module 03 · Concept

Drug discovery, step by step

1 · Target ID

Find a biological target (receptor, enzyme, pathway) linked to the disease.

2 · Hit finding

Screen thousands of compounds; keep the ones that bind the target.

3 · Lead optimization

Refine the best hits for potency, safety, and drug-like properties.

4 · Candidate + preclinical

Pick one molecule; run tox, pharmacology and ADME studies before humans.

Where regulatory comes in — from day one

RA helps shape the Target Product Profile, scans the competitive/regulatory landscape, and starts planning which regions and pathway make sense — long before any patient is dosed.

    Module 06 · Concept

    What is CMC (Chemistry, Manufacturing & Controls)?

    CMC proves the drug can be made — safely, consistently, at scale, every single batch.

    Drug substance

    The active ingredient itself: how it's synthesised and controlled.

    Drug product

    The finished dose form — tablet, injection — including formulation and packaging.

    Specifications & stability

    Quality limits the product must meet, and how long it stays within them.

    CMC data fills CTD Module 3 — and any change to how the drug is made later usually triggers a regulatory variation.

    Module 08 · Concept

    What is Regulatory Operations?

    If Regulatory Affairs decides what to say, Regulatory Operations makes sure it's built, formatted, and delivered exactly the way the agency's system requires.

    eCTD publishing

    Assembling documents into the electronic Common Technical Document structure agencies can ingest.

    Submission gateways

    Transmitting the dossier through the agency's official electronic portal (e.g. FDA ESG, CTIS).

    Lifecycle & hyperlinking

    Tracking every sequence submitted over a product's life, cross-referenced and navigable.

    Technical validation

    Checking the submission against the agency's technical specification before it's sent.

    Module 09 · Concept

    What is Regulatory Intelligence?

    The rules keep changing. Regulatory Intelligence (RI) tracks those changes and translates them into action before they become a problem.

    Guidance monitoring

    Tracking new and draft guidance documents from every relevant authority.

    Policy & precedent tracking

    Watching how agencies are actually deciding cases similar to yours.

    Competitive intelligence

    Following what's approved, when, and under what conditions — for similar products.

    Module 12 · Concept

    Why every commercial plan needs regulatory input

    Speed to market = revenue

    Every month of delay is a month of lost sales and patent life.

    Avoiding costly rework

    Pricing and reimbursement conversations can't start until approval is secured.

    Getting safety communication right protects patients — and reputation.

    Module 13 · Concept

    Why "approved" isn't the same everywhere

    An approval in one country doesn't automatically make a drug sellable in another. Regulatory affairs manages every local requirement that stands between approval and a patient actually getting the medicine.

    Local registration

    A separate application, reviewed by that country's own authority.

    Manufacturing site GMP

    The manufacturing site itself may need local certification, not just the product.

    Labeling & language

    Local language, local warnings, sometimes locally-required data.

    Pricing & reimbursement

    A separate dossier and negotiation, distinct from the regulatory approval itself.

    Local vigilance obligations

    Ongoing safety reporting to that specific authority, on its own schedule.

    Licence renewal

    Market access has to be actively maintained, not just won once.

    Module 14 · Concept
